Our Mission

The American Red Cross Delivery Service is committed to providing fast, reliable, and compassionate delivery solutions to support communities in need. We combine decades of humanitarian expertise with modern logistics technology to ensure critical supplies and medical products reach their destinations safely and on time.

Our team of trained professionals works around the clock to deliver emergency relief, medical equipment, blood products, and other essential items that save lives and support disaster recovery efforts.

Our Values
