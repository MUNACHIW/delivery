Detailed Process Overview

Step 1: Request

How to Submit Your Request:
  • • Visit our website and fill out the delivery form
  • • Call our 24/7 support team: 1-800-733-2767
  • • Use our mobile app for quick requests
  • • Email delivery details to: request@redcrossdelivery.org

Step 2: Confirmation

What Happens After Submission:
  • • Our team reviews your delivery requirements
  • • We verify item compatibility and special handling needs
  • • We provide a quote and estimated delivery time
  • • You receive a confirmation number via email/SMS

Step 3: Tracking

How to Monitor Your Delivery:
  • • Real-time GPS tracking on our website
  • • Receive SMS updates at key milestones
  • • View driver information and ETA
  • • Contact support with any questions

Step 4: Delivery

Delivery Completion:
  • • Professional driver delivers your items
  • • Signature confirmation collected (if required)
  • • Receipt and delivery proof provided
  • • Feedback survey for service improvement

Delivery Requirements

Required Information

  • Pickup Location: Full address and contact info
  • Delivery Location: Complete destination address
  • Item Description: Details and quantity
  • Special Instructions: Handling requirements
  • Contact Number: For driver communication
  • Preferred Date/Time: Delivery window preference

Restricted Items

We cannot deliver:

  • Hazardous materials (without proper licensing)
  • Explosives or weapons
  • Perishable food items (non-medical)
  • Uninsurable high-value items
  • Live animals
  • Please contact us for special cases
